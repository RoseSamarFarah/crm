What It Is

BeeOrder is the leading food and quick-commerce delivery platform in Syria, connecting users with restaurants, supermarkets and local retailers through a single app.

Founded in Damascus in 2016, it now operates in five cities with more than 1,000 restaurant and 280 grocery partners, and handled 373,179 orders in July 2026.

Roughly 75% of organised delivery in those cities runs through BeeOrder. The company has been profitable since 2024.

How It Works

A customer orders in the app. The order reaches the merchant and BeeOrder's dispatch system at the same time, and the system assigns the nearest suitable driver. Delivery takes 40 minutes on average.

Merchants pay a commission on each order and customers pay a delivery fee. Drivers ride their own vehicles, so capacity grows without capital.

Syria has almost no consumer card payments, so BeeOrder built its own settlement layer. Drivers pre-fund a digital wallet, pay the merchant on collection, take cash at the door, and clear their balance at designated cash points.

  2. Resilience through economic and political shocks

    The platform has traded through a decade of war, currency collapse and the 2024 political transition, and grew GMV in every one of the last four years. Orders through July 2026 are running 99% ahead of the same period in 2025.

  3. A competitive edge built on proprietary technology

    More than 90% of the stack is built in-house — user app, merchant dashboard, driver app, dispatch and CRM — so pricing, routing and promotions can be changed in days, not quarters.

  4. A business and cash model designed for Syria, not imported into it

    Driver-owned vehicles keep capacity asset-light, and a pre-funded driver wallet settles merchant and customer cash in a market with almost no consumer card payments.

  5. A market at an inflection point

    US, EU and UK sanctions lifted inside eighteen months and foreign capital is entering — the window to deepen penetration before international entrants arrive is now.

  6. A management team with standing in the market

    A founder-led team that has run the company through the whole of that period and is among the most visible voices in Syrian technology.
